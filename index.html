Kun-Ying Lee (李坤應)

I am a first-year undergraduate student at IIIS (Yao Class), Tsinghua University, majoring in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence Track).

Currently, I am a research intern at the MARS Lab at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), advised by Prof. Hang Zhao. My research interests lie broadly in Robotics and Embodied AI.

Additionally, I am a member of the TH-MOS (Tsinghua Master of Soccer) robot soccer team, working on Gait Control algorithms for competitive robots.

Outside of research, I am a passionate pianist and a member of the Tsinghua University Clavier Team. I also enjoy playing frisbee.

Human Resource Machine (Oct. 2025)

Developed a desktop reimagining of the puzzle game “Human Resource Machine” using C++17, SFML 3, and ImGui-SFML.

github.com/KunYing-Lee/HumanResourceMachine
Logisim Based RISC CPU Implementation (Jun. 2025)
  • Designed a 32-bit single-cycle RISC CPU using Logisim Evolution.
  • Implemented modular components including ALU, Instruction Decoder, Program Counter, and Register File.
  • Supported R-type (Arithmetic/Logical) and I-type (Immediate/Load/Control) instructions.
github.com/KunYing-Lee/Logisim-Based-RISC-CPU-Implementation
UAV-Based Food-Delivery Routing For Tsinghua University (Aug. 2025)
  • Applied A-star algorithm to generate safe and efficient UAV routes that avoid obstacles and follow constraints
  • Developed a web-based simulation interface that visualizes deliveries in real time
github.com/KunYing-Lee/UAV-Routing-for-Tsinghua
DualBot Explorer Project Development (Feb. 2023)

Developed a dual-agent conversational AI system employing Langchain, GPT-4o, FAISS and Streamlit. Implemented three interaction modes: Debate Mode, Paper Digest Mode and Peer-review Simulation Mode.

github.com/KunYing-Lee/DualbotExplorer
