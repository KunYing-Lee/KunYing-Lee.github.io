About Me

I am a sophomore undergraduate student at IIIS (Yao Class), Tsinghua University, majoring in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence Track).

Currently, I am a research assistant at the MARS Lab at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS), advised by Prof. Hang Zhao. My research interests lie broadly in Robotics and Embodied AI.

Additionally, I serve as Gait Control Group Lead and Decision-Making Algorithms Lead at TH-MOS (Tsinghua Master of Soccer), working on locomotion control and team decision-making for humanoid robot soccer.

I’m originally from Taoyuan, Taiwan, and grew up in Suzhou, Jiangsu. Outside of research, I enjoy playing the piano and am a member of the Tsinghua University Clavier Team. I also sing bass in the Yongheng Choir and enjoy playing frisbee.

Projects

|

ScaleBFM-K1: Large-Scale Humanoid Motion Tracking

Adapted ScaleBFM-based motion tracking to the 22-DoF Booster K1, achieving over 95% full-clip completion on the BONES-SEED test set after 5 hours of training on a single node with 8 NVIDIA A10 GPUs. Deployed and verified the policy on the real robot.

SuperMimic on mjlab: Model-Based Humanoid Motion Tracking

Reproduced SuperMimic’s differentiable-world-model approach to humanoid motion tracking on native MuJoCo-Warp through mjlab, achieving 100% full-clip completion across 40 LAFAN1 motions within 5 hours of training on a single RTX 5090. Demonstrated few-shot adaptation to highly dynamic MotionCode motions.

Omnidirectional Locomotion

Developed command-conditioned AMP policies for omnidirectional locomotion on Booster K1, achieving stable real-robot velocity tracking from −1.5 to +2.0 m/s forward/backward, ±1.5 m/s laterally, and ±1.7 rad/s in yaw.

Fall Recovery

Trained an AMP-based fall-recovery policy in MJLab for Booster K1, recovering from tested fallen poses to stable standing within 0.5 s and succeeding in all 30 real-robot trials.

Dribbling

Trained reference-free dribbling on Booster K1 using asymmetric PPO, jointly learning locomotion and head control from ball-velocity commands, proprioception, and camera-frame ball observations.

Shooting

Developed target-conditioned shooting policies on Booster K1 to aim at specified 3D goal-mouth coordinates from positions across a 14 × 9 m field. Real-robot tests achieved targeting errors within 10 cm in 27 of 30 trials; demonstrations include a goal scored at RoboCup.

Built a Unity–MuJoCo platform for autonomous 3v3, 5v5, and 11v11 humanoid soccer, connecting learned locomotion, kicking, and recovery with perception, team decisions, and match management.
